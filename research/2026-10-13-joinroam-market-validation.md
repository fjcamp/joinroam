# JoinRoam — Research checkpoint 2026-10-13-03
**Status:** RESEARCH / VALIDATION DESIGN. No production deployment, code changes on main, legal setup, or SII activity changes.

## Decision
Fora Travel is discarded as a business route. JoinRoam is treated as a candidate specialist product within the JoinHook ecosystem: connect travelers using campervans/motorhomes, vehicle rental businesses/private fleet owners, and owners of adequately prepared overnight spaces.

## Executive findings
- Chile has a meaningful tourism market, but total domestic overnight trips and international arrivals are not a proxy for RV rentals. Sernatur reported 62.4 million domestic overnight trips in 2025; this is all tourism, not camper-specific demand.
- Press reporting from Feb 2025 cited strong summer demand for campers/motorhomes, advance booking as far as six months, and daily prices in the CLP 130,000–240,000 range. This is an indicative historical market signal, not a verified 2026 price census.
- Direct fleet rental and peer-to-peer marketplaces already exist. JoinRoam should not compete as just another listings catalog. Differentiate through verifiable space-readiness, reliable comparable listings, local coordination, owner diagnostics, safety/amenity standards and evidence-based preparation plans.
- No official national dataset specific to annual camper/motorhome rental volume, fleet utilization, or total rental revenue was found in this initial research. Validate via operator interviews, fleet inventory, availability calendars, price sampling and traveler interviews.

## Critical insurance and risk findings
- SOAP is compulsory traffic injury/death insurance; it does not cover damage to the rental vehicle. Never describe SOAP as full vehicle insurance.
- Verify in writing: covered driver(s), own damage, third-party liability/property damage, theft, deductible/franchise, security deposit, glass/tires/roof/undercarriage, unpaved roads, border crossing, assistance/towing, exclusions, geographic restrictions, claim workflow and refund conditions.
- Yeti's published policy/terms report territorial exclusions related to provinces Malleco, Cautín and Arauco, and exclusions for prohibited/off-road use. Do not recommend or route a customer through those areas under that cover without written insurer/operator confirmation.
- Insurance inclusion is provider-specific. A marketplace may not insure the vehicle; do not infer protection from the fact a listing is bookable.

## JoinRoam business model: proposed multi-sided network
**Supply side 1 — vehicle rental companies / private fleet owners:** inventory, availability, season, daily rate, deposit, included kit, mileage, age/licence rules, insurance and deductibles, pickup/return, support, permitted travel zones and cancellation terms.
**Supply side 2 — overnight space owners:** landowner consent, access/road conditions, vehicle dimensions/turning radius, flat parking, capacity, water, toilets, grey/black-water disposal as applicable, solid waste, lighting, fire safety, emergency access, connectivity, quiet/privacy, booking/price rules, emergency contacts and seasonality.
**Demand side — travelers:** international/domestic origin, dates, party size, vehicle type, route, daily budget, required facilities, pickup/drop-off, insurance needs, driving/license readiness and consent to follow up.
Use explicit lead permission, data minimization, status, source, next action and follow-up date. Store private traveller documents only if necessary and with appropriate access controls.

## Space-readiness principle
Do not list every piece of land as a campsite. Create review tiers:
- Initial screening — ownership/permission, zoning/land use, access and no obvious fatal constraint.
- Minimum readiness — safe access/parking, vehicle clearance, reasonably level area, safe water and toilet solution, waste handling, emergency access/communications, fire safety and clear rules.
- Improved readiness — electricity or charging where appropriate, lighting, showers, disposal facilities, reliable connectivity, signage, booking/contact process and documented local services.
- Verified listing — evidence-backed photos, map access, capacity, season, prices, included facilities, restrictions, emergency info and a date-stamped field check.
This is an operational checklist, not a legal certification. Verify current municipal, health, land-use, construction, water, wastewater, waste, fire and SERNATUR requirements for each particular site. SERNATUR classifies camping/encampment establishments within accommodation registration; the specific obligations must be checked for the exact activity.

## Value proposition for JoinHook
The value is not “a map of RVs and campsites.” It is a data/evaluation and coordination layer:
1. Standardized, comparable vehicle and space profiles.
2. Evidence-backed minimum readiness status instead of unverified listings.
3. Match travelers with vehicle and overnight-space options by route, season, capacity, facilities and restrictions.
4. Reduce avoidable safety/insurance misunderstandings through explicit disclosure.
5. Help owners prioritize low-capital improvements before investing.
6. Produce territory-level insight about supply gaps, seasonality, vehicle availability and demand.
7. Use JoinHook's diagnostics/data workflows to support review, tracking, economics and later automation.
No claim of certification, guaranteed safety, legality, bookings or profitability without evidence.

## Validation gates and KPI
Phase A: inventory 30–50 suppliers/spaces/opportunities and collect 5–10 owner/operator interviews.
Phase B: interview 10–15 travelers, including RV users and people who considered renting but did not.
Phase C: manually test 1–3 paid owner diagnostics and/or a lead-matching pilot before building booking/payment systems.
Measure response rate, verified inventory, space readiness, willingness-to-pay, booking intent, time per diagnostic, cost to acquire a lead, quote-to-book conversion, completed booking/cancellation, issues with insurance disclosure and repeatability.
Move to software only after documented pain, repeatability, feasible service economics and clarified compliance.
