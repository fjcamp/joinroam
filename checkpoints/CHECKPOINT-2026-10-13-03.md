## Decision and product
Fora Travel is discarded. JoinRoam is developed as a candidate JoinHook product connecting:
1. Travelers using campervans, motorhomes and caravans.
2. Commercial/private vehicle rental providers.
3. Owners of suitable overnight spaces who meet JoinRoam's evidence-based minimum-readiness checks.

JoinRoam should not compete as a basic listing catalog. Its differentiator is verified information, space-readiness diagnostics, comparable vehicle/insurance disclosures, territorial coordination and owner-focused preparation services.

## Research observations
- Sernatur reported 62.4 million domestic trips with overnight stays in 2025; this is general tourism, not RV rental demand.
- Press reporting in Feb 2025 discussed seasonal RV/camper demand, advance bookings up to six months and example prices of CLP $130,000–$240,000/day. This is a dated market indicator, not a current complete census.
- Providers identified include Holiday Rent RV, Yeti Campers, Nomade Campers/Motorhomes, Chile Campers, Camper World Chile and marketplaces Travana/rollbnb. Prices, base locations, pickup points and insurance vary by model/date; check the linked research report before publishing any rate or claiming coverage.
- Initial service geography: Arica, Calama/San Pedro positioning, Colina/Santiago, Concón, Pucón (provider-dependent), Puerto Varas/Llanquihue, Coyhaique and Punta Arenas. A delivery point is not necessarily a permanent depot; regional inventory remains incomplete.
- No official national figure specifically identifying annual Chilean RV rental volumes, utilization or revenue was established in this initial research.

## Insurance / safety critical points
- SOAP covers personal injury/death in traffic accidents, not damage to the vehicle.
- Always validate own-damage, theft, third-party liability, deductible, deposit, tires/glass/roof/undercarriage, assistance, permitted drivers, international travel, prohibited roads and territory restrictions in writing.
- Yeti's published conditions flag exclusions involving Malleco, Cautín and Arauco and unauthorized/off-road use; obtain written provider/insurer confirmation before suggesting any route affected by restrictions.
- A marketplace is not automatically the insurer. rollbnb's published terms say it does not provide its own vehicle insurance.

## Space-readiness levels
- Level 0: do not publish; missing owner authorization, unsafe access, unresolved fatal issues or unsafe waste disposal.
- Level 1: self-contained overnight stay only; responsible host, access/vehicle compatibility, safe stable site, arrival info, emergency contact, disclosed lack of services and no untreated waste discharge.
- Level 2: verified basic facilities including toilets, privacy/hygiene, potable water if claimed, waste, capacity and transparent pricing.
- Level 3: motorhome-ready amenities; confirmed manoeuvring dimensions, electricity where provided, authorized grey/black-water disposal, emergency access and measured connectivity if work-from-anywhere is advertised.
These are internal quality tiers, not legal certification. Legal, municipal, health, zoning and Sernatur duties must be checked for each site.

## Business and customer portfolio
Maintain three separate CRM pipelines:
- vehicle rental providers;
- property/space owners;
- travelers and potential demand.
Record contact consent, source, last verification, next action/date and only minimum necessary personal data. Suggested first fieldwork: 10 vehicle providers, 10 space owners and 15 travelers; these are targets, not interviews completed.
